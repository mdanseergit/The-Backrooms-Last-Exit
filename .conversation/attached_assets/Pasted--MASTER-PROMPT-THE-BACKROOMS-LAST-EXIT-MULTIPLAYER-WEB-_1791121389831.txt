## 1. GAME OVERVIEW

Title: The Backrooms: Last Exit

Genre: Co-op survival horror, procedural levels, online multiplayer, browser-based (no install).

Premise: Players "no-clip" out of reality into the Backrooms. Squads must solve puzzles, collect power cells, and open the Exit Door of each Level while being hunted by four unique entities. Teamwork, voice chat, and reviving downed friends are core.

Players: 1–4 players per team. Up to 4 teams (max 16 players) per server instance.

Modes:

- CO-OP SQUAD: one team, 1–4 players, all vs entities.

- TEAM RACE: up to 4 teams race to exit each Level first. Entities hunt everyone.

- PRIVATE LOBBY: friends only, join via room code or invite.

## 6. THE FOUR ENTITIES (GHOSTS) THAT HUNT AND KILL

Each has a distinct AI behavior tree/state machine (Patrol → Investigate → Chase → Attack → Cooldown), distinct sensing, and counterplay. The server owns all AI.

1. THE HUM WARDEN (sound hunter) — tall, faceless janitor-like silhouette in a stained uniform. Hunts by SOUND: sprinting, voice chat, dropped items, doors. Blind in complete silence. Counter: crouch, whisper, throw noisemakers as decoys.

2. THE GRIN (light predator) — a floating smile in the dark that only moves when the player is NOT looking at it (line-of-sight freeze) and dwells near dead lights. Counter: flashlights, keep eyes on it, repair fuse boxes to restore lighting.

3. THE SEAM CRAWLER (wall/ceiling stalker) — long-limbed creature that travels through wallpaper seams and ceiling tiles, leaving subtle wall ripples and wet sounds. Ambush attacker that targets isolated players. Counter: stay grouped, watch for ripples, use salt/flare tools to seal seams.

4. THE MIMIC (deceiver) — copies a teammate's name tag, footsteps, and voice lines (recorded phrases) to lure players apart. Counter: password/handshake emotes, verify via ping wheel, tag-check scanner.

Death and revive system:

- Entity hit = DOWNED (not instant death) → bleed-out timer (45 s), crawl slowly, can ping teammates.

- A teammate holds interact for 5 s to REVIVE (interruptible; revive revives with 30% health). Limited revive tokens per team per level; Revive Kits can be found/crafted.

- If bleed-out ends, the player is DEAD and becomes a spectating "echo" who can help by pinging and flickering lights, and can be brought back at a rare Revive Altar for a big co-op puzzle reward.

- Entities can drag/carry downed players (a rescue mini-mission).

- Team wipe = level restart with a harder seed or return to checkpoint.

- Sanity meter: low sanity causes hallucinations, false audio, and Mimic gets stronger; teammates in proximity restore sanity.
